## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability within the GMEL System (including Firebase Firestore security rule flaws, authentication bypasses, or data exposure risks), please report it responsibly. **Do not disclose security vulnerabilities publicly until they have been patched and acknowledged.**

### How to Report
* **Email:** Send details directly to our Lead Architect at **info.enrichdata@gmail.com** with the subject line `[SECURITY VULNERABILITY] GMEL System`.
* **Phone / Secure Contact:** For urgent disclosures, contact EnrichData Tanzania support at **+255 758 216 364**.

### What to Include in Your Report
To help us triage and verify the issue quickly, please provide:
1. **Description:** A clear overview of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
2. **Steps to Reproduce:** A detailed proof-of-concept (PoC), script, or step-by-step instructions.
3. **Affected Components:** Specific views, modules, or API/Firebase rule boundaries involved.

### What You Can Expect
* **Initial Response:** Within **48 hours**, you will receive an acknowledgment confirming receipt of your report.
* **Status Updates:** You will receive regular status updates every **5 business days** while our engineering team investigates and develops a patch.
* **Resolution & Credit:** Once validated, we will deploy a secure patch to production and credit you in our release notes (unless you prefer to remain anonymous).
